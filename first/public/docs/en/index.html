The first post‑quantum standard

FIPS 203: ML‑KEM

FIPS 203 standardizes ML‑KEM (based on CRYSTALS‑Kyber), a key encapsulation mechanism based on modular lattices. It is the first NIST standard for post‑quantum key exchange, designed to resist both classical and quantum attacks.

Why it matters

NIST process and real-world impact

ML‑KEM won the 8‑year NIST competition, beating 82 candidates. It is already integrated in Chrome, Firefox, and Cloudflare. US federal agencies are required to adopt it by 2030 (CNSA 2.0), making it a global standard for future security.
